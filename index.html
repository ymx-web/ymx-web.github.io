About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Human–Computer Interaction Laboratory at the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ISCAS), and the Intelligent Emergency Laboratory at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), under the supervision of Prof. Ying Li and Dr. Jiaqi Zhu.

My research focuses on knowledge-enhanced large language models and intelligent agents, with particular interests in structured knowledge representation, knowledge reasoning and retrieval, and long-term agent memory. My recent work explores how structured knowledge and memory can support reliable reasoning and continuous evolution in LLM-based agents.

My broader goal is to bridge structured knowledge and large language models to build intelligent systems that can effectively represent, retrieve, reason over, and evolve knowledge during long-term interactions.

Research Interests

  • LLM Agents & Agent Memory — long-term memory, memory evolution, and continual agent learning
  • Knowledge Graphs & Hypergraphs — structured representation and graph/hypergraph-based knowledge modeling
  • Knowledge Reasoning — structure-guided reasoning and knowledge-enhanced large language models
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation — structured retrieval and knowledge-grounded generation
